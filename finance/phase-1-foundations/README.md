# Phase 1 — Foundations

> Core concepts: markets, trading, participants, data, and terminology.
> Build the mental model that all later phases depend on.

## Prerequisites

None. This is the starting point.

## Learning Outcomes

After this phase, you should be able to:

- Explain what finance, economics, and accounting are and how they differ
- Describe a company, its types (public, private, startup, unicorn), and its stakeholders
- Understand stocks, IPOs, exchanges (NSE, BSE, NASDAQ, NYSE)
- Identify market participants and their roles (retail, institutional, market makers, regulators)
- Explain how trading works: order book, bid/ask, spread, liquidity, settlement
- Differentiate market cap categories and understand free float
- Describe major indices (NIFTY 50, Sensex, S&P 500, Dow Jones, NASDAQ 100)
- Understand market cycles: bull, bear, correction, crash
- Distinguish investment types (stocks, ETFs, bonds, gold, REITs, crypto)
- List what drives stock prices (earnings, interest rates, inflation, news, sentiment)
- Evaluate company performance factors (earnings, debt, competition, management)
- Know where financial data comes from and which sources to trust
- Understand how AI is used in finance and where the field is heading
