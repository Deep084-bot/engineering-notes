# Glossary

> Quick reference for all finance terms introduced in Phase 1.

---

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Accounting** | Recording and reporting of past financial transactions. |
| **Accumulation** | Early phase of a market cycle where informed investors buy quietly. |
| **Annual Report** | Comprehensive yearly document with audited financials and strategy. |
| **Ask** | The lowest price a seller is willing to accept for a security. |
| **Asset Class** | A category of investments with similar characteristics (stocks, bonds, etc.). |
| **Bear Market** | A sustained decline of 20% or more from recent highs. |
| **Bid** | The highest price a buyer is willing to pay for a security. |
| **Bond** | A loan made by an investor to a company or government. |
| **Broker** | An intermediary that connects traders to exchanges. |
| **Bull Market** | A sustained rise of 20% or more from recent lows. |
| **Company** | A legal entity that exists separately from its owners. |
| **Correction** | A short-term market decline of 10% to 19%. |
| **Crash** | A rapid, severe market decline of 20%+ in days or weeks. |
| **Dividend** | A portion of company profits paid to shareholders. |
| **Earnings** | A company's profit after all expenses. Also called net income. |
| **Economics** | The study of how markets, incentives, and systems behave. |
| **Equity** | Same as stock. Ownership in a company. |
| **ETF** | Exchange Traded Fund — a basket of securities trading as one stock. |
| **Enterprise Value (EV)** | Market Cap + Total Debt - Cash. Total company value. |
| **Finance** | The study of capital allocation under uncertainty. |
| **Free Float** | Shares available for public trading (excludes promoter holdings). |
| **Hedge Fund** | A private, lightly regulated investment partnership for wealthy investors. |
| **Index** | A statistical measure tracking a group of stocks. |
| **Inflation** | A general increase in prices of goods and services. |
| **Institutional Investor** | A large organization managing billions of dollars. |
| **IPO** | Initial Public Offering — the first sale of shares to the public. |
| **Liquidity** | How easily a security can be traded without moving its price. |
| **Market Cap** | Stock Price × Outstanding Shares. Total equity value. |
| **Market Cycle** | The repeating pattern of accumulation, bull, distribution, and bear phases. |
| **Market Maker** | A firm that continuously quotes bids and asks to provide liquidity. |
| **Market Order** | An order to buy/sell immediately at the current best price. |
| **Market Participant** | Any entity that interacts with financial markets. |
| **Mutual Fund** | A professionally managed pool of money from many investors. |
| **NAV** | Net Asset Value — the per-unit value of a mutual fund. |
| **Order Book** | The list of all active bids and asks for a security. |
| **Primary Market** | Where new securities are sold for the first time (e.g., IPO). |
| **Private Company** | A company whose shares are not traded publicly. |
| **Public Company** | A company whose shares are listed on a stock exchange. |
| **Rally** | A short-term price increase during a longer-term decline. |
| **Recovery** | A sustained rise in prices following a bear market. |
| **Regulator** | A government body that oversees markets (SEC, SEBI). |
| **REIT** | Real Estate Investment Trust — a company owning income-generating real estate. |
| **Retail Investor** | An individual trading their own money. |
| **Secondary Market** | Where existing securities are traded between investors. |
| **SEC** | US Securities and Exchange Commission — the US market regulator. |
| **SEBI** | Securities and Exchange Board of India — the Indian market regulator. |
| **Settlement** | The final transfer of ownership after a trade (T+1). |
| **Share** | Same as stock. A single unit of ownership. |
| **Shareholder** | Someone who owns shares in a company (partial owner). |
| **Spread** | The difference between the ask price and the bid price. |
| **Stakeholder** | Anyone affected by a company's actions (superset of shareholders). |
| **Startup** | A young company designed for rapid growth. |
| **Stock** | A piece of ownership in a company. Also called share or equity. |
| **Stock Exchange** | A regulated marketplace for trading securities. |
| **Unicorn** | A private startup valued at $1 billion or more. |
| **Volume** | The total number of shares traded in a given period. |
