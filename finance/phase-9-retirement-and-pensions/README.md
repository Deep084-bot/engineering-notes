# Phase 9 — Retirement & Pensions

> How retirement income works in India: build a corpus, convert it into cash flow, and choose the right pension wrapper.

## Prerequisites

Phase 1 — Foundations
Phase 5 — Financial Metrics
Phase 8 — Portfolio & Risk Management

## Learning Outcomes

After this phase, you should be able to:

- Model how much corpus you need for retirement
- Understand defined benefit, defined contribution, and hybrid pensions
- Compare NPS, PPF, EPF/EPS/VPF, APY, UPS, SCSS, SSY, and NPS Vatsalya
- Navigate the NPS platform: eNPS, PRAN, CRA, PoP, PFM, and D-Remit
- Apply NPS contribution, exit, partial-withdrawal, and tax rules
- Build a retirement portfolio that combines growth, safety, and liquidity
- Avoid common retirement-planning mistakes
